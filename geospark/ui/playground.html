Playground

Ask, measure, explore.

Every result here is computed by real tools on open data, and comes with its proof: sources, licences, units and caveats. Download any result as GeoJSON for QGIS.

Ask in plain words

A language model picks GeoSpark's tools; the numbers come from the tools, not the model.

Reachable area

Where can you get to in a few minutes? Routing covers Australia; points elsewhere are refused rather than guessed.

Mode
Minutes (up to 4)

Open map data

Places, buildings, roads and boundaries from Overture Maps around any point on Earth. Distances and areas are geodesic.

Geodesic distance

The shortest distance over the WGS84 ellipsoid between two points.

Geocode

A place name to coordinates, with a warning when the name is ambiguous.

Elevation

Height above sea level from a digital elevation model, with its vertical accuracy.

Spatial relationship

Does A contain, touch or overlap B? Invalid geometry is refused, and swapped lat/lon is flagged.
